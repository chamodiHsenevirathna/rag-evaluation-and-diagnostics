# Fictional data and veterinary-dosing notice

All documents, companies, products, people, policies, prices and figures in this project's corpora, evaluation datasets and validation scenarios are **fictional** and were generated by an AI assistant for testing a software evaluation method. Any resemblance to real organisations, products or individuals is coincidental. Invented names are placeholders; no affiliation with or statement about any real entity is intended. The datasets are not included in this release.

**Veterinary content.** Some validation scenarios (a fictional veterinary clinic) contain dosing, frequency and treatment-style figures. They are **invented test data, not veterinary or medical guidance**. They have not been checked against any clinical source and may be wrong or unsafe. Do not use them to treat, dose or care for any animal; consult a qualified veterinarian. The same applies to the fictional building-permit and data-centre scenarios: they are not legal, regulatory or engineering advice.
